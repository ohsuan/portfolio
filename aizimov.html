AI PRODUCT · NAMING

AIZIMOV

Turn an open-ended naming brief into a globally trademarked name.

01

An Open-Ended Brief

The product was an AI-powered product that needed a name and a clear direction for how it should be presented. The initial requirements were simple:

A

Start with "A"

AI

Clearly indicate AI integration

TECH

Align with a technology-driven product image

With no single naming direction yet, I began by exploring different ways the name could be approached.

02

Exploring What the Name Could Be

I explored several naming territories:

Concept

Names built around ideas related to AI, creativity, and imagination.

AIdea AImage AImagine

Mascot

Names that could potentially work as a character or recognizable identity.

AIredale AI-Aye / Aye-AI

Names & Meanings

Names inspired by cultural and linguistic references, particularly those associated with technology, modernity, mystery, elegance, freedom, or creativity.

Altheira Athenix Astrell

Word Fusion

Blending meaningful words or concepts into a new name.

art + creation → Artion
art + inspira → Aspira
artistic + vista → Arista

Word Pairing

Exploring combinations of complete words that could be freely paired and rearranged.

A WORDS
art aurora ample
I WORDS
ink inspire ignite
A + I
Art Ink Ample Ink Aurora Ignite

During this stage, I explored variations within each territory and brought promising candidates to the stakeholder for discussion.

03

A New Reference

A new requirement was later introduced: the name should connect to Isaac Asimov.

ORIGINAL BRIEF A + AI + Technology
→
NEW REFERENCE Isaac Asimov
→
NEW QUESTION How could the name connect to Asimov?

The connection made sense for the product direction. Asimov is widely known for his science fiction, particularly his writing about robots and technology, making his work a natural reference for an AI-related product. His name also naturally contains the letters "A" and "I", connecting back to the original naming requirements.

So the exploration became:

How could we take inspiration from Asimov while creating a distinctive product name of our own?

I continued exploring the new direction, including:

Asimovition Asimo Asimovio Asimova Aimov
04

Bringing the Team In

For the next round, I led an ideation session with team leads and managers across marketing, design, and product.

During the session, I introduced the product concept and naming direction while encouraging the team to explore both Asimov-inspired names and alternative ideas. Strong non-Asimov concepts were welcomed, as they brought valuable cross-functional perspectives.

Combining my initial explorations with team proposals, we selected nine candidates for the vote:

Asimo AInnovation Asimova Nexus David Artify Artion AIgo AItify

To make the discussion more concrete, I introduced two criteria for evaluation:

Core Service

How intuitively does the name connect to the product?

Brand & Marketing

How much potential does the name have as a recognizable and marketable brand?

Each team member received three votes, allowing us to narrow the candidates down to three finalists:

9 candidates
→
3 finalists
05

Reframing Asimov

Although the session yielded three finalists, none strongly reflected the Asimov reference. The stakeholder requested a deeper exploration down that path. Rather than forcing a decision from a shortlist that didn't fully hit the mark, I revisited the Asimov reference.

I led a second round of team ideation and structured the direction into three approaches:

A

Asimov + Word

Joining or blending Asimov with another word to create a new expression.

B

Asimov + Concept

Combining Asimov with another meaningful word or concept.

C

Asimov-inspired Form & Sound

Exploring names that retained the sound, structure, or visual form of Asimov.

We generated a much larger set of candidates across the three approaches. To narrow them down, we ran a screening vote first.

28 ideas generated
→
15 screened candidates

The remaining 15 candidates were evaluated using the same criteria in a second round of voting.

Through the final vote, three candidates tied and became our finalists:

Asimofusion
Asimov Inspire
Aizimov
06

Recommending AIZIMOV

The second round left us with three finalists, each receiving the same number of votes: Asimofusion, Asimov Inspire, and Aizimov.

After reviewing the options, I formed a final recommendation. Aizimov, proposed by a designer under the form/sound category, stood out for how seamlessly it merged the original requirements with the Asimov reference.

AI

Creates an immediate connection to the product's AI foundation.

A–Z

Evokes a sense of completeness, breadth, and an end-to-end experience.

ASIMOV

Retains a recognizable connection to Isaac Asimov, bringing in associations with science fiction, robots, and future technology.

Together, these gave AIZIMOV a name that was not simply derived from Asimov, but could also carry its own meaning and positioning.

AI + A–Z + ASIMOV
AIZIMOV

I organized these ideas into a clear naming rationale and presented the three finalists to the stakeholder. I explained why I believed AIZIMOV offered the strongest combination of these ideas, and we moved forward with AIZIMOV as the primary direction.

3 FINALISTS Asimofusion · Asimov Inspire · Aizimov
→
RECOMMENDATION AIZIMOV
07

Making the Name Official

Once AIZIMOV was approved, I led the process to register and secure trademarks in both Taiwan and the United States. Securing both trademarks officially transitioned the name from an internal decision into a protected brand asset.

✓ Taiwan Trademark
✓ U.S. Trademark
✓ Domain

This marked the transition from a naming exercise into a concrete product asset that could support its future development and commercialization. I also secured the corresponding domain while retaining the other two finalists as backups.

BACKUP OPTIONS
Asimofusion Asimov Inspire

The naming process had moved from exploration and discussion to a name ready to move forward with the product.
