A New Portfolio

My portfolio v1 was shaped around my background in UX research.

Its visual identity was built around my initials, with an O around a centered H. It reflected how I work: looking closely at people, their thoughts, behaviors, and what lies beneath the surface.

As I brought these perspectives into product work, I wanted to build a new portfolio that felt closer to where I am now. Not just a new way to present my work, but a new way to present myself.
